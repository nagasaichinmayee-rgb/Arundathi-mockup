Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our programmes, departments, admissions, and patient care options.

Which is the best medical college in Hyderabad? +
AIMS — Arundathi Institute of Medical Sciences — is one of Telangana's premier NMC-approved medical institutions, known for its 1200-bed teaching hospital, 42 clinical departments, and strong MBBS pass rates exceeding 98%.
Does AIMS Hyderabad accept insurance? +
Yes. AIMS is empanelled with all major government and private health insurance providers, including Aarogyasri, CGHS, and leading private insurers.
How do I apply for MBBS at AIMS? +
MBBS admission is through NEET-UG and KNRUHS state counselling. Visit our Admissions page for seat categories, fee structure, and deadlines.
Does AIMS offer 24/7 emergency services? +
Yes. The AIMS Emergency & Trauma Centre operates 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, with dedicated teams in trauma, cardiac emergencies, and critical care.
Can I get a second opinion at AIMS? +
Yes. Book a Second Opinion consultation with any of our specialist faculty either in-person or via our teleconsultation platform.
What hostel facilities are available for students? +
AIMS provides separate, fully-equipped hostels for male and female students within the campus, minutes from the hospital. All rooms include Wi-Fi, mess facilities, and a common room.
