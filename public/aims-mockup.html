Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, treatments, appointments, and patient care options to help you make informed health decisions.

Which is the best hospital in Hyderabad? +
AIMS — Arundathi Institute of Medical Sciences — is one of Telangana's premier NMC-approved medical institutions with a 1200-bed teaching hospital, 42 clinical departments, and an MBBS pass rate exceeding 98%.
Does AIMS Hyderabad accept insurance? +
Yes. AIMS is empanelled with all major government and private health insurance providers, including Aarogyasri, CGHS, and leading private insurers.
How can I get a second opinion at AIMS Hyderabad? +
Book a Second Opinion consultation with any of our specialist faculty either in-person or via our teleconsultation platform.
Can I avail teleconsultation services at AIMS Hyderabad? +
Yes. AIMS offers teleconsultation across all major specialities. Book an online appointment from the comfort of your home.
Does AIMS Hyderabad offer 24/7 emergency services? +
Yes. The AIMS Emergency & Trauma Centre operates 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, with dedicated teams in trauma, cardiac emergencies, and critical care.
How can I find the best hospital near me in Hyderabad? +
AIMS operates multiple campuses across Hyderabad including Jubilee Hills, Secunderabad, Financial District, Hyderguda, and DRDO Kanchanbagh.
Does AIMS Hyderabad offer health checkups? +
Yes. AIMS offers comprehensive health check packages for all age groups and genders. Book your package online or call our helpline.
